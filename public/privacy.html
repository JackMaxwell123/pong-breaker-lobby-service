Pong-Breaker! Privacy

Current beta · Updated October 8, 2026

This policy describes the Pong-Breaker! game and the online service operated by PipeDream Interactive. For player support, privacy questions or data requests, contact PipeDreamInt@gmail.com. You do not need to install the app to contact us or delete a profile.

Offline play

Practice, settings and cached equipped designs stay on your device. Connecting to online play or syncing a collection contacts our service. The game does not request your contacts, microphone, camera or precise location.

Your online profile

We store a random player identifier, a one-way hash of your private recovery code, coins, owned and equipped designs, ratings, match results, reward history and operation records needed to avoid duplicate grants. We do not ask for your real name, email address or date of birth to create a game profile. A recovery code gives access to that profile; keep it private.

Connections and matches

Online play exchanges your chosen display name, equipped cosmetics, connection information and game inputs. The lobby and your opponent receive the information needed to connect. A direct peer connection can disclose your public IP address to your opponent. If direct connection fails, the free service can relay gameplay.

Public matches send input records to the service so it can verify results independently. Raw input uploads are held in service memory until verification or expiry, for at most about 20 minutes. The resulting match receipt and coin or rating changes are stored in the database. We do not use match inputs for advertising.

Hosting and support

Render hosts the online service and Neon hosts the database. These providers process network and infrastructure information, including IP addresses, under their own policies. The current service and database are configured in the United States. If you email support, Gmail processes the message and we use your address and message to respond to your request. Do not email your recovery code unless we have discussed a specific secure recovery procedure.

Ads, purchases and analytics

This beta has no live advertisements or paid purchases. Optional ad and purchase controls stay unavailable while their providers are unconfigured. We do not sell personal information, include third-party behavioral analytics, or show automatic advertisements. If live Google Play purchases or optional rewarded ads are introduced, this policy and the applicable store disclosures will be updated before activation.

Retention and deletion

Your profile remains available until you delete it. In the app, open Profile → Recovery & Privacy → Delete Online Profile. You can also use the web deletion tool with your recovery code. Deletion removes your coins, collection, rank, reward challenges and detailed coin ledger, and disables the recovery code. It is permanent.

A minimal inactive account reference, the unusable recovery-code hash, and pseudonymous match and transaction references remain to prevent duplicate rewards and resolve conflicting results. These fraud-prevention records currently have no automatic expiry. Infrastructure logs and provider backups can retain earlier records according to provider retention. We do not use retained deleted-account records to restore a playable profile. A restored operator backup must preserve completed deletion requests.

Your requests

Contact PipeDreamInt@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion, or to ask about retained information. We may need to verify that you control the profile. Without its recovery code or other verifiable evidence, we may not be able to identify or recover a random device profile. You can always play offline without one.

Changes

Material changes to data use will be described here and in the relevant app release. This page covers the beta service as it works today.
